# Mikhail Naugolnov

**Petroleum engineer & R&D lead — turning reservoir science into working digital tools.**

I work at the intersection of upstream geoscience, reservoir engineering and data science: from waterflood analytics and probabilistic economics to ML models running in production for geologists, petrophysicists and production engineers.

- 🛢️ **Now:** efficiency of end-to-end exploration processes (technology, data & process levers) and a portfolio of digital projects in exploration
- 🧪 **Before:** Head of Digital Lab at NIS (Serbia) · reservoir engineering & development analytics at Gazprom Neft (West Siberia)
- 🎓 PhD candidate · Saint Petersburg Mining University (petroleum engineering, economics & management)
- 📚 40+ publications · 3 registered software programs · 1 patent application
